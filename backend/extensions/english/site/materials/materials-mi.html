Clinical course and staged teaching

Patient intake · Teaching Points

Discuss the working diagnosis based on the history, ECG, and physical examination findings already obtained. Do not reason backward from unopened reports or later coronary angiography results. Case reference: Regional ST-segment elevation and ischemic symptoms support activating the emergency reperfusion pathway without waiting for peak troponin. Discuss Killip class only when the relevant physical signs have been obtained. Do not falsely claim that a type 1 mechanism was angiographically confirmed before the procedure.

Management and handover · Teaching Points

The case reference pathway comprises urgent coordination with the catheterization laboratory, antiplatelet loading, periprocedural anticoagulation, intensive lipid lowering, monitoring, and risk communication. Medications require verification of suitability and contraindications. The Student acts as the receiving physician and does not perform the intervention. All orders are recorded only as plans; execution must be confirmed through the workflow. Do not independently supply doses that have not been configured.

Ward round · Teaching Points

Continue dual antiplatelet therapy and lipid lowering after the procedure; monitor for recurrent ischemia, heart failure, arrhythmias, bleeding, renal function changes, and access-site complications. There is no other indication for anticoagulation; do not routinely continue heparin. Begin progressive rehabilitation once stable. Integrate the opened handover and reassessment materials; elevated troponin alone must not be equated with reinfarction.

Discharge date · Teaching Points

The case discharge plan includes medication reconciliation, adherence to antiplatelet therapy, smoking cessation, rehabilitation, follow-up in 7–14 days to assess symptoms, renal function, and potassium, repeat lipid testing in 4–6 weeks, and repeat echocardiography in 6–12 weeks. Explain emergency management for recurrent chest pain and bleeding. Stent placement alone does not establish a cure. Specific doses must follow the reviewed medical record; this exercise does not automatically generate prescriptions.

Feedback · Teaching Points

Review only the actual dialogue, reports opened, and medical record text. List strengths, areas not yet supported by evidence, and recommendations for next time. Do not provide numerical scores or pass judgments, or count undocumented actions as completed. State that this is formative feedback and that the case and scoring remain pending expert review.
